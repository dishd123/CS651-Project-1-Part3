ABOUT STUDYBOARD

We help students keep the parts of class that disappear.

A board captures the structure. A recording captures the reasoning. StudyBoard is designed to connect both.

OUR POINT OF VIEW

Learning is a story, not a stack of files.

Important context often lives in the moment: the aside that clarified a formula, the correction made after a diagram was drawn, or the question that changed the direction of a discussion.

Our planned StudyBoard experience brings those moments into one calm workspace that students can search, review, and question.
